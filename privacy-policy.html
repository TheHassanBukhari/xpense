Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Xpense does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter into the app stays on your device and is never accessible to the developer or any third party.

About This App

Xpense is a personal expense tracking app developed by Hassan Bukhari. It is a free, offline tool that helps individuals record and monitor their daily spending against a personal budget.

Data Collection

Xpense collects no data of any kind. The app does not have internet access and cannot transmit information even if it wanted to. There are no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no third party libraries that access user data.

Data Storage

All expense records, balance entries, budget settings, and preferences you enter into Xpense are stored exclusively on your device using Android's private app storage. This storage is sandboxed by the Android operating system, meaning no other app can read it and the developer has no access to it under any circumstances.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with any third parties because we do not have access to any data. There is nothing to share.

Data Deletion

All data stored by Xpense is deleted automatically and permanently when you uninstall the app from your device. You can also clear app data at any time through your device Settings under Apps. There is no account to delete and no server side data to request removal of.

Children

Xpense is a general purpose personal finance tool intended for users of all ages. The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children. It is compliant with applicable laws regarding children's privacy because it collects and transmits no data whatsoever.

Permissions

Xpense requests no special permissions from your device. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or any other system resource beyond its own private storage folder.

Disclaimer

Xpense is a personal record-keeping tool only. It does not provide financial advice of any kind. The accuracy of all records depends entirely on what the user enters. The developer is not responsible for any financial decisions made based on information tracked in this app.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new date. Given the nature of the app, significant changes are unlikely.
